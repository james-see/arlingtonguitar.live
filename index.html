Common questions

Do I need my own guitar?

No. A guitar is provided for you to use during in-person lessons.

When are lessons available?

Hours are flexible, daily including weekends. Call or text (703) 239-4344 to find a time.

Are lessons in person or online?

Lessons are in person in Arlington, with hands-on guidance.

I'm a total beginner. Is that OK?

Absolutely — beginners are welcome. Lessons start from tuning and first chords and build from there.
